# Offline guidebook content sources

Reviewed: 2026-11-27

The guidebook contains stable geographic and cultural descriptions, not live business data. Suggested stop sequences are editorial ideas, not computed routes. The app deliberately avoids publishing opening hours, ticket prices, live road status, or claims that a road is currently passable.

## Editorial constraints

- Seasonal recommendations are broad planning guidance, not forecasts.
- Road notes warn about known mountain-road risks but do not assert current conditions.
- Before travel, users must check official closures, protected-area visitor information, weather and local restrictions.
- Search terms are passed to the offline POI catalog. A guidebook entry does not guarantee that the place exists in the currently installed map extract.
