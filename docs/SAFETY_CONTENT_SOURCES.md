# Safety content sources

Reviewed: 2026-11-27

The in-app emergency and incident notes are intentionally short, conservative reminders. They are not a substitute for certified first-aid training, dispatcher instructions, or current official advice.

## Georgia emergency number

- Public Safety Command Center 112, official FAQ: https://112.gov.ge/?lang=en&page_id=1715
  - 112 is Georgia's unified emergency number for police, fire/rescue, and medical response.
  - The service operates 24/7 throughout Georgia; a call still requires mobile-network coverage.
  - The FAQ lists Russian among the languages handled by call-takers.
- Georgia Travel, medical information: https://georgia.travel/coming-to-georgia/medical-information

## Road-incident response and first aid

- UK Highway Code, Annex 7 — First aid on the road: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-highway-code/annex-7-first-aid-on-the-road
  - Protect the scene, call emergency services, avoid moving casualties unless there is immediate danger, do not remove a motorcyclist's helmet unless essential, keep casualties warm, and do not give food or drink.
- American Heart Association / American Red Cross, 2024 First Aid Guidelines: https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/2024-first-aid-guidelines
  - Treat abnormal responsiveness or breathing and severe bleeding as emergencies; activate emergency medical services.
  - Apply direct manual pressure for severe external bleeding.

The app does not automatically place calls or send coordinates. Users must explicitly open the dialer, messaging app, or share sheet.
